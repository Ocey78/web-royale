# Web Royale 0.58.12 — Frostbite Hero snowball barrage

Frostbite's Hero ability now rains 25 Giant Snowballs across its existing radius over four seconds, starting when activated. The snowballs use the normal Giant Snowball damage, slowing and knockback, with visible falling projectiles and impact effects.

The ability still costs 3 elixir, extends the field by four seconds, and adds exactly five Ice Spirits during that extension. Its single-use restriction remains. Frostbite still costs 10 elixir.

The coordinated Hero Mega Minion Horde ability from 0.58.11 is included: one 2-elixir activation for all surviving members, different targets with wraparound, and one control per Horde.

No tests, simulations or gameplay/browser checks were run at the user's request. Build and publication records are under docs/qa/v05812/.
