# Web Royale 0.58.9 — Frostbite extension and faster evolved Lightning

**Frostbite now costs 10 elixir.** Its Hero ability costs 3 elixir and extends the active field by four seconds, summoning five additional Ice Spirits evenly over those added seconds. It replaces the previous Snowball barrage. The normal spawning schedule continues until its original end, followed by the five bonus Spirits; the Hero controller remains alive through the extension.

**Lightning Storm Evolution now strikes much faster:** its first wave lands after 0.25 seconds and the remaining waves arrive 0.3–0.6 seconds apart. It still has eight waves, strikes every eligible enemy in range and grows its radius by 20% each wave after the first. The ordinary Lightning Storm keeps its existing timing. Lightning Storm remains 10 elixir with a two-cycle Evolution, and CODE RED remains 14 elixir.
