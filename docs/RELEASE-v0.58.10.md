# Web Royale 0.58.10 — Sandbox Evo and Hero controls

Sandbox now has **Evo** and **Hero** checkboxes for the selected card. Each is available when that card has a supported form. Selecting one clears the other; clearing both returns to the standard card. Evolutions deploy immediately without cycling, and form placement uses the selected Sandbox level.

The controls work with normal and Ultimate cards, including Ultimate spell Evolutions and Hero Frostbite. Form-specific models, abilities and effects load before placement. Hero ability controls operate on the selected blue or red side and retain normal ability costs, charges and cooldowns.

Tests and gameplay/browser checks remain skipped at the user's request. Build and publication records are under `docs/qa/v05810/`.
