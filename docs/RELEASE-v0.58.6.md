# Web Royale 0.58.6 — Bowler Giant bridge clearance

Bowler Giant's collision radius is reduced from 1.05 tiles to 1 tile. His previous 2.1-tile body exceeded the two-tile bridges used by Ultimate Clash 1v1 and 2v2, leaving no legal crossing position. The existing navigator supports one-tile-radius bodies on bridge centerlines.

His model and both boulders keep their 40% enlargement. Health, damage, targeting and card cost are unchanged.

Tests and gameplay/browser checks were skipped at the user's request. The required website build and publication record are under `docs/qa/v0586/`.
