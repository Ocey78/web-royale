# Web Royale 0.58.2 — Ultimate expansion

All three Ultimate Clash deck builders now share 30 exclusive cards. The ten additions include custom portraits, descriptions, battle graphics and deterministic replay support.

| Card | Elixir | Behavior |
|---|---:|---|
| Mortar Battery | 12 | Three Mortars in a triangle, with staggered opening shots and their usual minimum range. |
| Royale Recall | 4 | Returns up to three nearby friendly troops to safe positions on your side. Health, shields and harmful statuses remain; they pause briefly after arrival. |
| Goblin Magnet | 5 | A temporary building pulls nearby enemy ground troops without dealing damage. |
| Spirit Festival | 7 | Sixteen Spirits arrive as Fire, Ice, Electro and Heal pairs, repeated over eight seconds. |
| Dark Pact | 4 | A seven-second field grants friendly troops 40% more damage while draining 2.5% maximum health each second. The drain cannot kill; overlapping fields do not stack. |
| Battle Kitchen | 8 | Throws healing food to injured friendly troops. Its Chef emerges on destruction and fights with a frying pan. |
| Royale Reinforcements | 12 | Six Royal Recruits arrive in pairs immediately, after three seconds and after six seconds. |
| Elixir Spell | 2 | A six-second purple field reduces ground movement speed by 40% for both teams. It follows the proposed Elixir Spill concept and does not grant elixir. |
| Dragon King | 18 | A large flying dragon with a two-second windup and a broad fire cone. Attack resets interrupt its breath preparation. |
| Bowler Giant | 11 | A Bowler and two boulders enlarged by 40%. He only selects buildings as targets, but both rolling boulders hit enemy ground troops along their paths. |

The earlier 20 card prices, three Hero/Evolution choices, team timers, twenty-elixir cap, normal tower health displays and protected Bomber translucency remain intact. New cards are isolated from ordinary decks and progression.

Prices are initial design values for playtesting, not a claim of competitive balance. Native actors are reused with custom visual additions. Team battles continue to use AI teammates; this release does not add online human matchmaking.

New Ultimate replays fingerprint the expanded content and include custom projectile state. Older Ultimate recordings with the previous pool are rejected as different content; existing normal replay fixtures remain compatible.

Verification and deployment evidence are recorded under `docs/qa/v0582/`.
