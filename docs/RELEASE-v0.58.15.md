# Web Royale 0.58.15 — Practice cheats and Taunt Tower

Settings → Practice Cheats now has two independent toggles:

- **Ultimate cards in normal decks** adds the Ultimate pool to ordinary deck editors. You can mix normal and Ultimate cards, save the deck and play it. Ultimate cards use level 11 unless a practice-level override is selected. Turning this off replaces Ultimate cards in ordinary decks while preserving the remaining slots and dedicated Ultimate decks.
- **Half elixir costs** halves your card deployment costs, retaining fractions such as 1.5 elixir. Decks, card details, the hand and the next-card preview show the discounted costs. Mirror calculates its usual price before applying the discount once. Hero ability costs are unchanged.

With Ultimate access enabled in a normal game, your maximum elixir is 20 when Half elixir costs is off, or 10 when it is on. Opponents retain their normal capacity and costs. Dedicated Ultimate, Twenty Elixir and Uncapped Elixir modes retain their own caps. Existing practice rules exclude cheat matches from rewards and AI training; saved replays retain the cheat settings.

Ultimate Musketeer keeps her 4-elixir base cost and Princess range. She now fires 50% more often (one attack every two thirds of a second) and sends two full-damage bullets at her target per attack, with separated muzzle positions so both shots are visible.

**Taunt Tower** joins the Ultimate pool for 6 elixir, bringing it to 35 cards. This gold-and-white Inferno Tower has 12-tile range and up to six simultaneous beams on different enemies. Each beam ramps its own damage. Enemies being beamed focus on the tower when they can target buildings, and the taunt ends when the beam is lost or the tower is disabled or destroyed. Original card artwork and the in-arena model use the gold-and-white design.

No tests, simulations or browser gameplay checks were run, as requested. The required website build and publication records are in docs/qa/v05815/.
