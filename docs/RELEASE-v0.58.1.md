# Web Royale 0.58.1 — Ultimate teams and balance

Ultimate Clash now includes 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 in Events. Each has its own saved deck builder, with the same 20 exclusive cards, Hero/Evolution choices and 20-elixir cap for every player. Team modes use the existing AI teammates, team arenas and victory rules.

| Mode | Regulation | Overtime | Elixir changes, elapsed time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1v1 | 4 minutes | 3 minutes | 2x at 2:00, 2.5x at 3:00, 3x at 4:00, 4x at 6:00 |
| 2v2 | 5 minutes | 4 minutes | 2x at 2:30, 2.5x at 3:45, 3x at 5:00, 4x at 7:30 |
| 3v3 | 6 minutes | 5 minutes | 2x at 3:00, 2.5x at 4:30, 3x at 6:00, 4x at 9:00 |

Tiebreakers start after overtime: 7:00, 9:00 and 11:00 respectively. Team modes are also available in the Learning Center.

The exclusive card pool has a new price balance based on actual troop value, army commitment, repeated damage, utility and risk. See [every price and its rationale](ULTIMATE-BALANCE-v0.58.1.md). Ordinary card prices and progression are unchanged. Hero Frostbite's ability still costs 3 elixir.

Evolved Princess defenders retain the normal Princess Tower health frame, numeric HP and level badge. Their evolved attacks and eight-times tower health remain intact; King Towers still have ten-times health.

Bomb Squad's protected Bombers now use the Archer Queen ability's translucent appearance. They remain untargetable while their Guard lives and become fully visible and targetable when it dies.

New replays support all team seats and the extended match clock. Normal replays from 0.58.0 remain compatible. Older Ultimate recordings with the previous card prices are explicitly rejected as differing content instead of being played with incorrect costs.

Verification and deployment evidence are recorded under `docs/qa/v0581/`.
